What is RELAY?

An honest, private voice companion for Windows 10 & 11.

  • Hands-Free Computer Control: Launch multi-step tasks like "open yt, and go to shorts in yt", switch apps, or close windows with natural speech.
  • True Screen Awareness: RELAY reads dialogs, focused buttons, and text fields without vomiting background UI clutter.
  • 100% Private Offline Core: All basic desktop commands, speech recognition, and screen reading run directly on your laptop.
  • Fast Conversational AI: Ask general questions like "who invented the telephone" and get direct spoken answers in seconds.

Who is it for?

Built with extreme empathy for everyday humans.

  • Blind and Visually Impaired Users: Full desktop independence with clear delta narration, spoken confirmation, and reliable keyboard shortcuts.
  • Hands-Free Workers & Multitaskers: Cook, sketch, study, or write while controlling media, timers, notes, and searches without touching the mouse.
  • Anyone Tired of Complex Menus: Skip navigating nested settings or tiny buttons—just press to talk and let RELAY do the work.
  • Students & Professionals: Dictate paragraphs, run quick calculations, and summarize text on screen on the fly.

How to use RELAY

Two simple ways to talk. Zero background noise. Direct, predictable results.
